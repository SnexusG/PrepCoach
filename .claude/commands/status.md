Run `npx prep stats` and `npx prep weakness list`, then summarise in ~8–12 lines:

- overall DSA rating, label, 7d / 30d trend;
- sheet completion % with the easy / medium / hard split;
- strongest and weakest topics (mark low-confidence ones with "*"), and any progression suggestion
  (move up to hards / drop to easies) from `npx prep context`;
- active and improving weaknesses (the top 3 by times seen), plus what was resolved recently;
- reviews due today and in the next 7 days;
- one concrete recommendation for what to focus on next.
The dashboard at http://localhost:5173/ shows the charts.
